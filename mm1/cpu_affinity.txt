Affinity: 
"virsh capabilities" on Host => will show the cpu , core & numa details

xml file:

example 1:
<vcpu placement='static' cpuset='0-15'>16</vcpu>    ==> 16 vcpus contained within first phys cpus 0-15 

example 2:
 <vcpu placement='static' cpuset='0-15,32-47'>42</vcpu>  
   ==> cpus 0-15 & 32-47 are on first socket
   ==> SMP enabled, cpu ids 0 & 32 are SMPs (siblings) of core 1
 
example 3:

  <vcpu placement='static' cpuset='0,32'>4</vcpu>  ==> single core affinity (0 & 32 are siblings on a core)

After starting guest with cpu affinity, run:  virsh vcpuinfo mohan_x2avic_1


qemu command:

-cpu host,affinity=0-3
