Enabling KDB on the system:
############################
in /etc/default/grub, set :

GRUB_CMDLINE_LINUX="crashkernel=auto rhgb quiet ekgdboc=kbd earlyprintk=vga console=tty0 console=ttyS0,115200n8"
GRUB_CMDLINE_LINUX="crashkernel=auto rhgb quiet ekgdboc=kbd earlyprintk=vga console=tty0 console=ttyS0,115200n8"

then run:

grub2-mkconfig -o /boot/grub2/grub.cfg
